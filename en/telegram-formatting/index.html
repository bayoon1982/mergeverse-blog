In short

  • Telegram has no font setting; it has formatting: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, monospace, spoiler, quote and links.
  • The quickest way is to select the words in the message field and pick a style; Telegram Desktop adds Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+K and Ctrl+Shift+M (Cmd on a Mac).
  • Typing **bold** or __italic__ also works in the Android app and Telegram Desktop.
  • Fancy fonts from generators are Unicode maths characters, not formatting, and screen readers and search may not read them.
  • A bot can send up to 4,096 characters per message and 1,024 per media caption; a Premium rich text post holds up to 32,768.

To bold text in Telegram, type your message, select the words and choose Bold from the menu that pops up. On a computer, Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on a Mac) does the same, and in the Android app or Telegram Desktop you can type two asterisks on each side, like this. Italic, underline, strikethrough, monospace, spoiler, quote and links work the same way. Telegram has no font setting, so "fonts" from generators are something else, covered below.

    How to format text in Telegram

    Telegram's FAQ describes one method that covers every style: select text in the message field and pick the formatting. The styles it lists are bold, italic, underlined, strikethrough, spoiler, monospace, a link and a quote.

    1. Type the message, but don't send it yet.
    2. Select a word or part of a sentence.
    3. On a phone, pick the style in the menu that appears over the selection. On a computer, right-click the selection and open Formatting.
    4. Send the message as usual.

    Styles can be combined, so bold italic is fine. Telegram Desktop added that in June 2021. To strip formatting from a selection in Telegram Desktop, press Ctrl+Shift+N.

    Telegram keyboard shortcuts for bold, italic and links

    Formatting shortcuts arrived in Telegram Desktop 1.3.0 on 1 June 2018. The version history lists Ctrl+B for bold, Ctrl+I for italic, Ctrl+K to create or edit a link on the selected text, Ctrl+Shift+M for monospace and Ctrl+Shift+N to clear formatting. On a Mac, use Cmd in place of Ctrl.

    You may come across shortcuts for underline, strikethrough or spoiler in other guides. We could not find them in any official Telegram note, so the table shows a dash there rather than a guess.

    Typing symbols: asterisks, tildes and bars

    The older method works without the menu. A hint in the Android app spells it out: two asterisks on each side for bold (**text**), two underscores for italic (__text__), two tildes for strikethrough (~~text~~), a backtick for monospace (`text`), two vertical bars for a spoiler (||text||), and [text](url) for a link.

    Telegram Desktop has accepted asterisks and underscores since July 2017, and backticks even earlier. Three backticks before and after a passage make a code block. The Desktop history does not mention tildes or bars, so on a computer the menu is the safer choice for strikethrough and spoilers.

    Bots use a different syntax called MarkdownV2. There a single asterisk means bold and a single underscore italic, which is why the last column of the table differs from the typing column. That syntax is for developers sending messages through the Bot API, not for people typing in the app.

    Spoilers and quotes

    A spoiler hides the selected words in the chat, in the chat list and in notifications. The reader taps it to reveal the text.

    Quote formatting can go on any text, not only on someone else's message, and one message can hold several quotes. A long quote can be collapsed: format the passage as a quote and tap the collapse button in the message field. Readers expand it if they want to read it, and scroll past it if they don't.

    What "Telegram fonts" from generators really are

    Sites that offer Telegram fonts do not change any font. They swap ordinary letters for look-alike characters from the Unicode block called Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols, U+1D400 to U+1D7FF. It holds bold, italic, script, Fraktur, double-struck and other versions of Latin letters, some Greek letters and digits. The generator above does the same and shows you every style at once.

    Before you use them, know the downsides:

    • The Unicode Standard says these characters are meant only for mathematical or technical notation, not for ordinary text.
    • Each one has its own name, such as MATHEMATICAL BOLD SMALL A, so a screen reader may spell a styled word letter by letter or skip it.
    • Search that looks for ordinary letters may not find a styled word, in a chat or anywhere else.
    • Only Latin letters and some digits exist in the block. Cyrillic, accented letters and most punctuation stay as typed.

    The block also has gaps. There is no italic small h, for example: the chart points to the Planck constant sign ℎ instead, and some script, Fraktur and double-struck capitals are borrowed from a neighbouring block the same way. The generator fills those gaps exactly as the Unicode chart says.

    Fancy fonts in your name and username

    Not in your username. Telegram's FAQ allows only a-z, 0-9 and underscores there, so look-alike characters will not be accepted. Our page on the Telegram username has the full rules and the link formats.

    People do sometimes put styled letters in their display name. Keep in mind that anyone searching for you by name in ordinary letters may not find you, which matters if you want people to reach you through Telegram search. Telegram's own ways to decorate a profile, such as the gifts you choose to show, are covered in how to hide Telegram gifts, which also explains how to pin the ones you want on display.

    Telegram character limits

    The Bot API gives exact numbers for bots: 1 to 4,096 characters in a message and up to 1,024 in the caption of a photo, video or file. The count is taken after formatting is parsed, so the asterisks and bars of the markup don't count. Telegram publishes no separate limit for messages people type.

    For long posts, Telegram Premium users get a rich text editor with headings, tables and lists. Telegram's FAQ says one such message can hold up to 32,768 characters. Premium itself is explained in our Telegram Premium guide.

    The counter in the tool checks your text against all three numbers. If the text contains styled characters, it warns you: Telegram's documentation measures formatted text in UTF-16 units, and each of those characters takes two, so you may hit a limit sooner than a plain count suggests.

    Stickers and custom emoji are the other way people dress up messages. Our guide to Telegram stickers covers those.

    Questions people ask

    How do I bold text in Telegram on a phone?

    Type your message, select the words, and choose Bold in the menu that appears over the selection. In the Android app you can also wrap the words in two asterisks on each side, like **this**, before sending.

    What is the Telegram shortcut for bold?

    In Telegram Desktop it is Ctrl+B, or Cmd+B on a Mac. Ctrl+I makes italic, Ctrl+K adds a link to the selected word, Ctrl+Shift+M makes monospace and Ctrl+Shift+N clears formatting. These were added in version 1.3.0 in June 2018.

    Can I change the font in Telegram?

    Not the font of your messages. Telegram lets you style parts of a message (bold, italic and so on) but has no choice of typeface. Font generators replace letters with look-alike Unicode symbols instead, which is a different thing.

    How many characters can a Telegram message have?

    Telegram's Bot API allows 1 to 4,096 characters in a bot's message and up to 1,024 in a photo or video caption, counted after formatting is applied. Telegram publishes no separate number for people. The rich text editor for Telegram Premium users holds up to 32,768 characters in one message.

    Can I use fancy fonts in my Telegram username?

    No. A username accepts only a-z, 0-9 and underscores, according to Telegram's FAQ. Look-alike characters from a font generator will not be accepted there.
