In short

  • One gift: My Profile, Gifts, tap the gift, hide it. You can show it again the same way.
  • New gifts: Settings, Privacy & Security, Gifts lets you choose which gifts appear on your profile automatically.
  • Your name on a gift you send: choose to hide it when sending. The recipient still sees who sent it.
  • Hiding does not delete a gift. Only a regular gift converted into Stars within 7 days disappears for good.
  • Telegram describes no setting that hides gifts from chosen viewers only.

To hide a Telegram gift, open My Profile, go to the Gifts tab, tap the gift and choose to hide it from your profile. It stays on your account, and you can show it again the same way. To stop new gifts from appearing on your profile by themselves, open Settings, Privacy & Security, Gifts and choose which gifts are displayed.

How to hide one gift from your profile

  1. Open your profile (My Profile).
  2. Go to the Gifts tab.
  3. Tap the gift you want to hide.
  4. Choose to hide it from your profile.

Telegram's April 2025 post lists what you can do with any single gift from that window: show or hide it on your profile, pin it to the top of your list, wear it as your status, or transfer it to another user or a blockchain wallet. The last two apply to collectibles.

To bring a hidden gift back, open the same tab, tap the gift and choose to show it. Telegram's API documentation describes both actions as one switch: a gift is either displayed or not.

How to stop new gifts from appearing automatically

Hiding gifts one by one gets tedious if people send you many. The privacy setting handles new ones:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security.
  3. Tap Gifts.
  4. Choose whose gifts are displayed on your profile.

Telegram's April 2025 post gives examples such as showing only gifts from your contacts or from certain people. Telegram's API documentation calls it the rule for saving received gifts to the profile automatically. Gifts that do not match it still reach you; they just are not displayed until you choose to show them.

On Telegram Desktop the same section is under Settings, Privacy and Security, Gifts. Telegram's bug tracker refers to it in a report fixed in Desktop version 6.4.0.

Can you hide gifts from certain people?

Telegram documents no such option. The Gifts privacy setting chooses which senders' gifts appear on your profile. It does not choose who can see them. A gift you hide is hidden from everyone who visits your profile; a gift you show is visible to everyone who can see your profile.

If you have come across guides promising per-viewer hiding, check whether they describe the sender setting above. We could not find a per-viewer option on any Telegram page as of 30 October 2026.

How to stop receiving gifts at all

In the same Gifts section, Telegram Premium users can pick which gift types others may send them. Telegram's post gives the example of popular accounts accepting only unique or limited-edition gifts. Telegram's bug tracker names the block of switches "Accepted gift types". Turn them all off and others cannot send you gifts of those types.

The same section has a switch for the gift icon in your message bar. Telegram's API documentation says the gift button shows in a private chat when both people have it switched on, so turning it off removes the shortcut from your chats. It does not block gifts.

Hiding your name when you send a gift

When you send a gift, you can choose to hide your name. Telegram's October 2024 post is careful about what that means: the recipient still sees who sent it, but other people viewing the gift on the recipient's profile do not. Staying unknown to the recipient as well is not an option Telegram's post describes.

Your online status has its own privacy setting; here is what Telegram's "last seen recently" means. Collectible gifts can also be shared to a story, and Telegram stories explains who sees one and for how long.

Does hiding a gift delete it?

No. A hidden gift is still on your account. Telegram documents no way to delete a received gift. The only way a regular gift disappears for good is if you convert it into Stars, which is allowed within 7 days of receiving it (Telegram's API documentation, checked 30 October 2026). Our guide on converting Telegram gifts into Stars has the details.

A collectible can leave your account in other ways: you can sell it, transfer it or move it to the blockchain. A regular gift you want to sell later has to be upgraded to a collectible first.

Showing gifts off instead

Some people want the opposite: fewer gifts on view, but the good ones up front. Telegram has two tools for that:

  • Pinning. Since March 2025 you can pin up to 6 gifts to the top of your Gifts tab and your profile cover (My Profile, Gifts).
  • Collections. Since July 2025 you can group gifts into your own collections: open the Gifts tab, select a gift and tap Add Collection. Telegram's API documentation lists a limit of 10 collections per profile and 500 gifts in each (checked 30 October 2026).

If you are not sure what a gift is or what the different kinds can do, start with our page on what Telegram gifts are.

Questions people ask

How do I hide gifts on my Telegram profile?

Open My Profile, go to the Gifts tab, tap the gift and choose to hide it from your profile. Repeat for each gift. To stop new gifts from appearing automatically, open Settings, Privacy & Security, Gifts.

Can I hide Telegram gifts from certain people?

Telegram's posts describe no such option. The Gifts privacy setting decides whose gifts are shown on your profile, not who can see them. A hidden gift is hidden from everyone who views your profile.

Does hiding a gift delete it?

No. A hidden gift stays on your account and you can show it again. A regular gift disappears only if you convert it into Stars, which is possible within 7 days of receiving it.

Can I stop people from sending me gifts?

Telegram Premium users can choose which gift types they accept, under Settings, Privacy & Security, Gifts. Telegram's April 2025 post describes this as a Premium feature.

Can I hide my name when I send a gift?

Yes. When sending, choose to hide your name. Other people viewing the gift on the recipient's profile will not see who sent it, but the recipient will.
