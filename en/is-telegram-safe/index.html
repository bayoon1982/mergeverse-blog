In short

  • All Telegram chats are encrypted in transit, but only secret chats and calls are end-to-end encrypted; normal cloud chats and groups are stored encrypted on Telegram's servers.
  • Telegram's privacy policy says it may keep metadata such as your IP address for up to 12 months and may give your IP and phone number to authorities on a valid court order in a criminal case.
  • Most account losses come from people handing over a login code. Telegram says it never asks for your codes or your 2-Step Verification password.
  • Turn on 2-Step Verification, check Active Sessions, and use secret chats for anything sensitive.

Telegram is reasonably safe for everyday chats if you know what it does and does not protect. Every message is encrypted on its way to Telegram's servers, but only secret chats and calls are end-to-end encrypted. Ordinary chats, groups and channels are stored encrypted in Telegram's cloud. The bigger risk for most people is account theft through a shared login code, which a few settings make much harder.

Everything below comes from Telegram's own FAQ and privacy policy, checked on 20 November 2026. We are not affiliated with Telegram, and we are not a security firm: if your safety depends on your messages staying private, get advice from people who work on that for a living.

Which Telegram chats are end-to-end encrypted?

Telegram has two kinds of private conversation. Cloud chats are the default: your one-on-one chats, every group and every channel. Secret chats are a separate type you start on purpose. Telegram's developer documentation describes them as one-on-one chats encrypted with a key held only by the chat's participants.

Chat type Encryption Where it lives
One-on-one cloud chat Client-server encryption Telegram's servers, synced to all your devices
Group or channel Client-server encryption Telegram's servers, synced to all your devices
Secret chat End-to-end (client-client) Only the two devices that started it
Voice and video call End-to-end Processed on the two devices

Telegram's FAQ says all data, text or media, is encrypted the same way, using 256-bit AES, 2048-bit RSA and Diffie-Hellman key exchange. Its privacy policy adds that the contents of calls are processed only on your device and your recipient's.

The trade-off is convenience. Secret chats are not in the cloud and can't be opened on other devices, while cloud chats follow you to any device. If you compare messengers on this point, our page on Telegram vs WhatsApp puts both companies' own statements side by side.

What a secret chat does differently

Secret chats leave no copy on Telegram's servers. Telegram's FAQ lists the differences:

  • Messages can't be forwarded out of a secret chat.
  • When you delete a message on your side, the other app is told to delete it too.
  • You can set a self-destruct timer on messages, photos, videos and files.
  • The chat exists only on the device where you started it. Log out and it's gone.

To start one, open the person's profile, tap the menu (…) and choose Start Secret Chat. To check that nobody sits in the middle, compare the encryption key picture in the chat settings with your friend's, ideally in person.

Screenshots are the weak spot. Telegram says it tries to notify you about them, but some systems can't detect them reliably, and nothing stops someone photographing a screen with another phone.

What Telegram can see and what it may share

For cloud chats, Telegram says it stores data in several data centers controlled by different legal entities in different jurisdictions, with the decryption keys split up and kept away from the data. The FAQ says that, to that day, it had disclosed 0 bytes of user messages to third parties, including governments.

The privacy policy adds two things worth knowing:

  1. Metadata. To fight spam and abuse, Telegram may collect metadata such as your IP address, devices and apps you've used, and your history of username changes. If collected, it is kept for 12 months at most.
  2. Court orders. If Telegram receives a valid order from judicial authorities confirming you are a suspect in a criminal case that breaks its Terms of Service, it may disclose your IP address and phone number after a legal review. It says such cases are listed in a quarterly report at t.me/transparency.

On advertising, the policy says Telegram does not use the contents of private chats or your contact list for ad targeting. Sponsored messages appear in public channels with 1,000 or more subscribers, in bots, stories and search, and Premium users don't see them.

How people actually lose Telegram accounts

Telegram's FAQ says the phone number is the only way it identifies a user: whoever has the number and the code has the account. That's why scams aim at the login code.

Login codes for your account arrive in the chat named Telegram, a verified profile with a blue check, or by SMS. Telegram states that it will never ask for your 2-Step Verification password or any verification code. Treat anyone who asks for one as someone trying to take your account.

A second chat, Verification Codes, carries codes for outside websites and apps. If a code shows up there that you didn't request, tap it and choose Report.

Settings that make Telegram safer

Telegram's FAQ places these in Settings > Privacy and Security, except the device list, which is also under Settings > Devices.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification. After that, a login needs both the code and your password. You can add a recovery email.
  2. Add a passkey if your device supports it. It lets you log in with your device PIN, fingerprint or face scan instead of an SMS code.
  3. Check active sessions. Under Devices (or Active Sessions) you can end any session you don't recognize.
  4. Set a passcode lock on the app, so someone holding your unlocked phone can't read your chats.
  5. Hide your phone number. By default only your contacts see it; you can narrow that further under Phone Number. Our guide to hiding your phone number on Telegram goes through the options.
  6. Decide who sees your last seen time. The trade-offs are explained on our page about last seen recently.

If your phone is stolen and you are still logged in on another device, Telegram's advice is to turn on 2-Step Verification there and end the old session. If you have no other device, ask your carrier to block the old SIM and issue a new one, then log in and end the old session.

Strangers, spam and scam messages

Our page on Telegram usernames explains how a public username changes who can find you.

When someone outside your contacts writes first, Telegram shows a page about them with their country, when they joined and the groups you share. You can block and report from that same screen. Telegram's apps also have a Report button that sends illegal content to its moderators. If an account is limited for spam, Telegram's spam FAQ says it can still message people who saved its number and reply to anyone who writes first. If someone blocked you instead, our page on being blocked on Telegram covers the signs.

Telegram's FAQ also warns against jailbroken or rooted phones, which make it easier for an attacker to take full control of the device and weaken app-level protections.

Are bots, channels and gifts a risk?

Bots are made by outside developers, not by Telegram. Telegram says a bot sees your public name, username, profile picture, language and what you send it, but not your phone number or other chats unless you share them. Its advice is to treat bots as strangers and never give them passwords, codes or bank details. More in our guide to Telegram bots.

Public Telegram channels can be opened by anyone, so check the blue verified badge before trusting one that uses a brand name. Be just as careful with anyone who offers a prize or Telegram gift and asks for your login code: Telegram says it never asks for one, and neither should anyone else.

Questions people ask

Is Telegram end-to-end encrypted?

Only partly. Secret chats and calls are end-to-end encrypted. Ordinary one-on-one cloud chats and all groups and channels use client-server encryption, so they are stored encrypted on Telegram's servers and sync across your devices.

Can Telegram read my messages?

For secret chats, Telegram says it cannot, because only the two devices hold the keys. For cloud chats, Telegram stores the data encrypted and says the decryption keys are split and kept apart from the data, but it is not end-to-end encryption.

Does Telegram share data with the police?

Its privacy policy says that on a valid order from judicial authorities confirming you are a suspect in a criminal case, it may disclose your IP address and phone number, and that such cases go into a quarterly transparency report.

Is Telegram safe from hackers?

The usual way in is not breaking the encryption but getting your login code. Turn on 2-Step Verification in Settings > Privacy and Security, never share codes, and end sessions you don't recognize.

Are Telegram bots safe?

Treat any bot as a stranger. Telegram says bots see your public name, username, profile picture and what you send them, but not your phone number or other chats unless you share them.
