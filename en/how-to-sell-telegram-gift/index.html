In short

  • Only a collectible (upgraded) gift can be put up for sale. Open it from your profile, tap Sell and set a price in Stars.
  • A regular gift cannot be listed. You can convert it into Stars, and only within 7 days of receiving it (Telegram's API documentation, checked 30 October 2026).
  • Telegram's documentation lists the seller's share as 80% of the price for a sale in Stars and 90% for a sale in TON.
  • A gift can carry a date before which it cannot be listed; the app shows it.
  • Stars on your own balance cannot be sold or withdrawn under Telegram's terms.

You can sell a Telegram gift only if it is a collectible, an upgraded gift. Open it from your profile, tap Sell and set a price in Stars; it then appears on Telegram's gift marketplace. A regular gift cannot be listed at all. What you can do with it is convert it into Stars, and only during the first 7 days after you receive it.

Which gifts can you sell?

Telegram has two kinds of gifts, and only one of them can be sold. If you are not sure which kind you hold, our page on what Telegram gifts are explains the difference in more detail.

Regular gift Collectible gift
How you get it Someone sends it to you for Stars You upgrade a regular gift, or buy one on the marketplace
Can be listed for sale No Yes, for Stars or TON
Can be converted into Stars Yes, within 7 days of receiving it No
Can be moved to the TON blockchain No Yes

So the first question is always the same: is this gift upgraded? A collectible has a number and a set of traits (model, backdrop, symbol). A regular gift has neither. If yours is regular and you want to sell it later, upgrade it first. The upgrade costs Stars unless the sender already paid for it.

How to sell a collectible gift on Telegram

These steps come from Telegram's blog post of 8 May 2025, which launched the marketplace. The post does not name an app version or a platform. The labels below are the ones Telegram used; your app may word them slightly differently.

  1. Open your profile and go to the Gifts tab.
  2. Tap the collectible you want to sell.
  3. Tap Sell.
  4. Set the price in Stars and confirm.

The gift then shows up on the marketplace, and your profile marks it with a Sale label. Changed your mind? Open the gift again and tap Unlist. Telegram's API documentation also lists a price of zero as the way to take a gift off sale, so apps treat "no price" as "not for sale".

When someone buys it, the gift moves to the buyer and the Stars land on your balance. You never hand anything over by hand.

How much of the price do you keep?

Telegram's API documentation sets out how the seller's share is counted. For a sale in Stars, the seller receives the price multiplied by 800 and divided by 1000, which is 80%. For a sale in TON the figure is 900 per mille, so 90%. The same page lists the allowed price range for a sale in Stars: from 125 to 100,000 Stars.

We checked these values on 30 October 2026. They sit in Telegram's client configuration, which the server sends to the app, so Telegram can change them without a new blog post. The app shows the amount you will receive before you confirm, and that is the number to trust.

We do not publish what gifts sell for. Prices on the marketplace change every day, and any figure here would be out of date by the time you read it.

Can you sell a gift as soon as you get it?

Not always. A collectible can carry a date before which it cannot be listed (the documentation calls the field can_resell_at). The same goes for sending it on to someone else (can_transfer_at) and for moving it to the blockchain (can_export_at). Telegram's documentation does not say how long these waits are, and we found no official page that does. Guides that quote a fixed number of days are repeating older figures.

In practice: if Sell is missing or greyed out on a collectible, open the gift and look for a date. Waiting is the only way past it.

Selling a regular gift: converting it into Stars

People often say "sell" when they mean this. A regular gift can be traded back for Stars, which go to your balance. Telegram's API documentation says the gift is permanently destroyed, and the Stars are added to your balance. It is allowed only within a set time after you receive the gift. The documented value is 604,800 seconds, which is 7 days (checked 30 October 2026).

You usually get back less than the sender paid. Telegram's documentation says the amount equals the full price only when the gift was bought with recently purchased Stars. The step-by-step version, with what to do once the week has passed, is in our guide to converting Telegram gifts into Stars.

One side effect that few guides mention: Telegram's FAQ says converting gifts into Stars lowers your profile rating.

Taking an offer instead of listing

Since December 2025, buyers can make an offer on a collectible straight from its owner's profile, in Stars or TON. According to Telegram's blog, the buyer's payment is reserved for as long as the offer runs and is refunded in full if you decline or the offer expires. When you accept, the gift moves to the buyer automatically. The app also shows how close the offer is to the gift's estimated value.

Telegram's API documentation says an accepted offer is charged the same commission as a resale. Offers can run for 6, 12, 24, 36, 48 or 72 hours (documented in seconds, checked 30 October 2026).

Selling outside Telegram

A collectible can be moved to the TON blockchain. Telegram's January 2025 post gives the path: My Profile, Gifts, pick the gift, then Transfer and Send via Blockchain. The API documentation adds that this needs your two-step verification password and opens a Fragment page. From there the gift can be traded on NFT marketplaces outside Telegram. Our page on Telegram NFT gifts explains what changes once a gift is on the blockchain.

We do not walk through outside marketplaces or trading bots here. Telegram's protection covers sales and offers made in the app; deals made elsewhere are between you and whoever is on the other side.

Can you turn the Stars into money?

Not from a personal balance. Telegram's Stars terms say users may not sell, withdraw or transfer Stars from their balance beyond the uses the terms list. You can spend them inside Telegram: on other gifts, on digital goods in bots and mini apps, on reactions and subscriptions. Our explainer on what Telegram Stars are lists those uses. Withdrawal to TON through Fragment exists only for owners of channels and bots, and only for Stars those channels and bots received.

How to avoid getting scammed

Most losses happen outside the app, so the rules are short:

  • Sell through Sell or an offer in the app, where the gift and the Stars change hands at the same moment.
  • Never send a gift first on a promise to be paid later.
  • Do not give anyone your login code or your two-step verification password. Moving a gift to the blockchain asks for that password, so whoever has it can take your gifts.
  • Ignore messages from "buyers" who ask you to open a link and connect a wallet.

Questions people ask

Can you sell Telegram gifts?

Yes, if the gift is a collectible, that is, an upgraded one. You list it on Telegram's own marketplace for Stars or TON. A regular gift cannot be listed; you can only convert it into Stars within 7 days of receiving it.

Why is there no Sell button on my gift?

Most often the gift is a regular one, and Sell exists only for collectibles. A collectible can also carry a date before which it cannot be resold. Upgrade the gift first, or wait for that date.

How much of the price do I get when I sell a gift?

Telegram's API documentation lists 800 per mille for sales in Stars and 900 per mille for sales in TON, so the seller receives 80% or 90% of the price. These are documented values checked on 30 October 2026; Telegram can change them.

Can I sell a Telegram gift for real money?

Inside Telegram a gift sells for Stars or TON. Stars on a personal balance cannot be sold or withdrawn under Telegram's Stars terms. We do not cover selling through chats or third-party bots: Telegram does not protect those deals.

What is the cheapest and the highest price I can set?

Telegram's documentation lists 125 Stars as the minimum and 100,000 Stars as the maximum resale price (checked 30 October 2026). We do not publish market prices: they change every day.
