A Telegram group is a chat where every member can write, and it can hold up to 200,000 people. You join a group through an invite link or, if it's public, by finding it in Telegram search. Admins run it with fine-grained rights, bans, anti-spam tools and optional topics that split one big chat into threads. Anyone can create a group for free in a few taps.

The facts below come from telegram.org and core.telegram.org, checked on 31 October 2026. We are not affiliated with Telegram, and we don't list groups to join.

What a Telegram group is

A group is a shared conversation. Unlike a channel, where only admins post, every member can send messages, reply, react and share files, unless the admins limit that. Messages show the sender's own name.

Telegram's FAQ lists what makes groups work at scale:

  • Replies and mentions. Someone who is replied to or mentioned gets a notification even if they muted the group, unless they muted that person.
  • Search inside the chat, with a filter by sender.
  • Pinned messages at the top of the screen. Members get a notification about a pin even when the group is muted.
  • Files of any type up to 2 GB each, or 4 GB with Premium.
  • Voice chats that members can drop into whenever they want.

Under the hood, the groups you create today are what Telegram's developer documentation calls supergroups. Older "basic groups" could hold only 200 members, and Telegram's apps upgrade them automatically when an admin uses a feature that needs a supergroup. You usually won't notice the switch.

What if 200,000 isn't enough? Telegram's documentation describes gigagroups, which have no member cap but where only admins can write, so they behave much like a channel. The groups tour gives the plain advice: if you're doing something massively popular, start a channel. Our guide to Telegram channels explains how they differ.

Public and private groups

A public group has a short link like t.me/publictestgroup. Anybody can open it, read the whole chat history and join to post. Links to single messages in a public group open for anyone, even without a Telegram account.

A private group has no public link. You get in when a member adds you or when you open an invite link. Admins can revoke an invite link at any moment, and Telegram says it stops working immediately.

Admins also decide whether new members can read earlier messages. Telegram's FAQ calls this persistent history and says any group can toggle it.

How to join a Telegram group

  1. Get a link. Most people join through a t.me invite link sent in a chat or posted on a website. Open it in Telegram.
  2. Or search. Type the group's name or username into the search box above your chat list. Only public groups appear in global results. For how that box works, see our page on Telegram search.
  3. Join. Some groups let you in right away. Others ask you to send a join request, which an admin, or since June 2026 an AI bot appointed as admin, reviews.

Inside, you can mute the group. Telegram's FAQ says a muted group still notifies you when people mention you or reply to you. Joined groups count toward your limit of 500 channels and supergroups, or 1,000 with Premium, as of 31 October 2026.

We don't link to group directories. They aren't run by Telegram, and nothing on them proves a group is what it says. Be careful with groups that promise easy money or ask you to move to a bot and type a login code.

How to create a group and invite people

Telegram's FAQ gives these steps:

  1. On iOS, start a new message from the icon in the top right corner of Chats. On Android, tap the round pencil button in the chat list. In Telegram Desktop, open the menu in the top left corner.
  2. Choose New Group.
  3. Add your contacts, or find people by username.
  4. Give the group a name and a photo.

To bring in people who aren't in your contacts, go to Group Info, then Add Member, then Invite to Group via Link. Anyone with Telegram can join through that link. To make the group public, give it a public link (a username) in the group's settings.

Since August 2026, a group can have a welcome message that only new members see. Open the group's profile and choose Edit, then Welcome Message. Groups with 100 or more members can also set an official sticker set that everyone can use in that chat without adding it; our page on Telegram stickers covers sets in general.

Admin tools for running a group

A group with more than a handful of people needs admins. Telegram's FAQ shows where to add them: on iOS, Group Info, then Edit, then Administrators; on Android, Group Info, the pencil icon, then Administrators; in Telegram Desktop, the three-dot menu, Manage group, then Administrators.

Each admin gets only the rights you give them. Telegram lists adding users, managing messages, blocking members, editing group info and adding new admins.

The rest of the toolkit:

  • Default permissions. Restrict all members from sending certain kinds of content, or from writing at all.
  • Partial bans. Put one member in read-only mode, or stop them sending stickers or media for a set time.
  • Recent Actions. A log of admin actions from the last 48 hours, visible to admins only. It also shows messages deleted and the original versions of messages edited in that period.
  • Aggressive Anti-Spam. Telegram's own anti-spam for large groups; as of 31 October 2026 it needs at least 200 members.
  • Hidden member list, for groups with at least 100 members as of 31 October 2026.
  • Admin bots. Bots can moderate automatically. Since June 2026 AI bots can be made admins to screen join requests.

Members can also boost a group with their Premium subscription. Telegram's FAQ says boosts turn on features for everyone, such as posting stories, custom emoji packs and voice-to-text. Paid features inside chats, such as paid reactions and paid messages, use Telegram Stars, which have limits of their own in the same client configuration.

Topics and communities

Large groups get messy fast. Telegram added topics in November 2022 to fix that. A group with topics works like a forum: each topic is its own chat with separate history, shared media and notification settings. Admins turn topics on in Group Settings and choose in Permissions who may create and manage them. Since November 2023, the apps remember whether you view such a group as topics or as one stream of messages.

Communities go one step further. Since July 2026, several groups, channels and bots can be linked into a community around one subject. Members see the other chats and can join them without an invite link, and the whole community can sit in your chat list as one item. Chats in a community can be visible to all members or hidden from everyone except community admins and that chat's own members.

Leaving a group without breaking it

An ordinary member can leave any time; the group carries on. Owners have more to think about.

Since February 2026, Telegram shows an owner a confirmation window when they leave, where they can pick a new owner on the spot. If they don't, ownership moves automatically to one of the admins after one week. Owners can also transfer ownership to another member at any time without leaving.

Deleting your whole Telegram account is different. Telegram's FAQ says the groups you created are left without a creator, but admins keep their rights and members can keep chatting. Gifts and other items tied to your profile follow their own rules; see what Telegram gifts are if that matters to you.

Questions people ask

How many members can a Telegram group have?

Up to 200,000, according to telegram.org. Channels have no subscriber limit, so for a one-way audience larger than that Telegram suggests a channel.

How do I join a Telegram group?

Open an invite link someone sends you, or search for a public group by name or username and join it. Some groups ask you to send a join request first.

Can group members see my phone number?

Not by default. Telegram's FAQ says your number is visible only to people who have you in their contacts, and you can change that in Privacy and Security settings.

What are topics in a Telegram group?

Topics split a group into separate threads, each with its own history, shared media and notification settings. Admins switch them on in the group settings.

What happens if the owner leaves a group?

Since February 2026, Telegram asks the owner to pick a new owner when leaving. If no one is picked, ownership passes to one of the admins after one week.
