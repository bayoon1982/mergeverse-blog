In short

  • A Telegram gift is an animated item, bought with Stars, sent to a person or a channel with an optional note.
  • The recipient can show it on the profile, hide it, convert it into Stars within 7 days, or upgrade it to a collectible.
  • Collectibles get a unique number and traits and can be worn, sold, sent on, crafted or moved to the TON blockchain.
  • Some gifts are limited, some only for Premium users, and new releases since November 2025 are sold by auction.
  • To send one: open the person's profile, tap ⋮ or ⋯, then Send a Gift.

Telegram gifts are animated digital items that you buy with Telegram Stars and send to another user or a channel, with a short note if you like. The recipient can show the gift on their profile, keep it hidden, trade it back for Stars within 7 days, or, if the gift allows it, upgrade it into a collectible with its own number and traits. Telegram launched them in October 2024.

Gifts in one paragraph

Telegram's October 2024 post introduced gifts as a way to congratulate contacts on holidays and occasions. Each one is an animation plus your message. You pay in Telegram Stars, the recipient gets it in the chat, and it can then sit in the Gifts tab of their profile. Since then Telegram has added collectibles (January 2025), a marketplace (May 2025), auctions for new releases (November 2025) and crafting (February 2026).

Kinds of gifts

Telegram's API documentation and blog describe these kinds:

Kind What sets it apart
Regular gift Bought for Stars at a fixed price; can be shown, hidden, or converted into Stars within 7 days
Limited gift Has a fixed total supply; once it sells out, it can no longer be bought new
Premium-only gift Only users with Telegram Premium can buy it
Auction gift New releases since November 2025 are sold through rounds of Star bids; the top bidder in round 1 gets gift #1
Collectible A regular gift after an upgrade: unique number, model, backdrop and symbol; can be sold, sent on and moved to the blockchain

There is also Telegram Premium sent as a gift, from the same Send a Gift screen, but that is a subscription, not an item on your profile.

Gifts bought at auction cannot be converted into Stars, according to Telegram's documentation. For limited gifts, the documentation records the size of the first supply and, for sold-out ones, the date of the first sale.

What are Telegram gifts for?

For the person who receives one, Telegram's posts list these uses:

  • Show it on your profile, in the Gifts tab, or keep it out of sight. Our guide on hiding Telegram gifts covers the settings.
  • Pin up to 6 favourites to the top of the tab and your profile cover, or sort them into collections.
  • Convert a regular gift into Stars within 7 days, losing the gift. The details are in our guide to converting gifts into Stars.
  • Upgrade it to a collectible, if the gift allows that.

Collectibles add more:

  • Wear one as your emoji status; your profile takes on its design.
  • Use it for a chat theme. Since August 2025 owning certain collectibles (Telegram named Plush Pepe, Precious Peach, Durov's Cap and Heart Locket) unlocks a chat wallpaper; one gift unlocks one theme for one chat.
  • Sell it on Telegram's marketplace or take an offer; see how to sell a Telegram gift.
  • Send it on to someone, combine it with others in crafting, or move it to the TON blockchain, where it becomes an NFT in your wallet. Our page on Telegram NFT gifts explains that step.

How to send a gift

From Telegram's posts:

  1. Open the person's profile.
  2. Tap the menu button (⋮ or ⋯, depending on your device), then Send a Gift.
  3. Pick a gift, add a message if you want, and choose whether to hide your name.
  4. Pay in Stars.

You can also start from Settings, Send a Gift and choose the recipient there. For a channel, Telegram's January 2025 post says to tap the gift icon at the bottom corner of the channel. When sending, you can include the upgrade so the recipient gets the collectible version free.

Telegram's API documentation caps the message at 128 characters (checked 30 October 2026).

A gift is not a sticker, even though both are animated art you send in a chat. Stickers come in sets, and ordinary ones cost nothing to send; we cover them in Telegram stickers.

Gifts are sent in chats, so it helps to know the kinds of chats Telegram has: our guides to Telegram channels and Telegram groups explain both, and Telegram bots covers the bots that take payments in Stars.

Where to find the gifts you have

Every gift you have received is under My Profile, in the Gifts tab, including the ones you hid. That is where Telegram's posts send you to view, sort, pin, hide or wear them. Gifts sent to a channel appear in the channel's own Gifts tab.

Can you buy a gift for yourself? Yes: Settings, Send a Gift, then pick yourself as the recipient. Telegram's bug tracker describes exactly that route in a Telegram Desktop report fixed in version 6.4.0.

What are resale gifts?

They are collectibles that their owners have listed on Telegram's marketplace. In May 2025 Telegram said to apply the Resale filter on the gift screen to see them; its August 2026 post calls the filter Collectible. Buying one gets you an existing collectible with its number and traits, instead of a new regular gift. Since August 2026 buyers can add a comment when sending a marketplace gift to someone ("signed gifts").

We do not discuss marketplace prices. They change daily, and the conversation quickly turns into guessing which gift will be worth more. The app shows the current price of each listing.

Are Telegram gifts worth money?

They cost Stars, and Stars cost money, but a gift is not a payment method. A regular gift has no buyer; its only exchange is back into Stars within the week. Collectibles can be sold for Stars or TON. And the Stars on a personal balance cannot be sold or withdrawn under Telegram's terms. Treat gifts as what Telegram built them for: something to give.

The famous ones

A few gifts are known by name well beyond Telegram, and searches for them are common. The best known is Plush Pepe.

Questions people ask

What are Telegram gifts used for?

To congratulate someone and to decorate a profile. Received gifts can be shown on the profile, converted into Stars within 7 days, or upgraded to collectibles, which can be worn as a status, used for chat themes, sold or sent on.

Are Telegram gifts real money?

No. They are digital items bought with Stars, and Telegram's terms do not let you sell or withdraw the Stars on your own balance. Only collectibles can be sold, inside Telegram for Stars or TON.

Do you need Telegram Premium to send or receive gifts?

No, with one exception: Telegram's API documentation describes gifts that only Premium users can buy. Choosing which gift types you accept is a Premium setting.

What are resale gifts on Telegram?

Collectible gifts that their owners have put up for sale on Telegram's marketplace. When sending a gift you can filter for them (Telegram's posts call the filter Resale, later Collectible).

Do Telegram gifts expire?

Telegram documents no expiry for gifts. What runs out is the option to convert a regular gift into Stars: 7 days after you receive it.
