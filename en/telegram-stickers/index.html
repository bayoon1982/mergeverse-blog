In short

  • A Telegram sticker is an image, a vector animation or a short video, kept in sets that anyone can add for free.
  • Static stickers are PNG or WEBP, animated ones use Telegram's .TGS format, video stickers are WEBM files up to 3 seconds.
  • Premium adds stickers with full-screen effects and lets you add and use custom emoji sets; everyone can see both.
  • You can make stickers from a photo in the built-in editor, or upload files through the @Stickers mini app.
  • Removing a set only removes it from your list; only the creator can delete a set for good.

Telegram stickers are pictures you send in place of a message: a still image, a vector animation or a short looping video. They come in sets, and anyone can add a set for free, make their own or share one with a link. Telegram launched stickers in January 2015. Premium adds stickers with full-screen effects, but ordinary stickers cost nothing to send.

Everything below comes from telegram.org and core.telegram.org, checked on 31 October 2026. Telegram changes its apps often, so where its pages give no exact menu path, we say so rather than guess.

What a Telegram sticker is

A sticker is a small piece of artwork that sits in your chat like a message. Stickers live in sets, and each sticker in a set is tied to one or more emoji. That link matters: when you type an emoji in a chat, Telegram suggests matching stickers from your sets.

The platform is open. Telegram's first custom sets arrived in May 2015, when artists could submit their own sets through the @stickers bot, and each set got a permanent link. The Premium FAQ still lists "tens of thousands of stickers" among the features that stay free for everyone.

The kinds of Telegram stickers

One set can mix several kinds, according to Telegram's API documentation. Here is what each kind is and what Telegram requires of the file, as of 31 October 2026:

Kind File Main requirements
Static .PNG or .WEBP One side exactly 512 px, the other 512 px or less
Animated .TGS (Lottie vector animation) 512×512 canvas, up to 3 seconds, looped, 60 FPS, up to 64 KB
Video .WEBM with the VP9 codec One side exactly 512 px, up to 3 seconds, up to 30 FPS, up to 256 KB, no audio
Custom emoji Any of the three formats Exactly 100×100 px

Animated stickers arrived in July 2019. Telegram built the .TGS format so that each one weighs about 20–30 KB, six times smaller than an average photo, and plays at 60 frames per second. Making them takes Adobe After Effects and Telegram's Bodymovin-TG plugin.

Video stickers came in January 2022 as the easier route. Any video editor that exports .WEBM will do, and showing them needs Telegram 8.5 or later.

Premium stickers

When Telegram Premium launched in June 2022, it brought dozens of stickers with full-screen animations. Premium users can send them in any chat, and the effect plays for everyone who sees the message, subscribed or not. Telegram said its artists would add to this collection every month.

In the sticker panel, Premium stickers from all your sets sit in their own section, below "Recently Used". The developer documentation adds one more detail: a Premium sticker can only be sent by a Premium user, but the animated effect plays for all users.

Custom emoji

Custom emoji arrived in August 2022, with version 8.9. They are made the same way as stickers, only smaller: 100×100 pixels. Anyone can create a custom emoji set. Adding and using those sets is a Premium feature. Still, all users can see custom emoji in messages, and anyone can try them out for free by sending them to their own Saved Messages chat.

How to add Telegram stickers

There are three common ways in.

  1. From a chat. Someone sends you a sticker from a set you don't have. Tap it, and Telegram offers to add the whole set.
  2. From a link. Every set has its own t.me link. Open it and add the set.
  3. From search. Since May 2026 the search in the sticker panel covers more than 100 million emoji and stickers made by Telegram users, with keywords in 36 languages.

Once a set is added, its stickers show up in the sticker panel and in the suggestions when you type a matching emoji. Telegram also has a separate list of featured sets it showcases in the app.

How to make your own Telegram stickers

The quickest way is the built-in Sticker Editor, added in April 2024:

  1. Open any chat and open the sticker panel from the message field.
  2. Tap the (+) button.
  3. Pick a photo. Tap once to cut out an object and remove the background; you can erase or restore parts by hand.
  4. Add text, a drawing, emoji, existing stickers or GIFs, and an outline if you want the classic sticker look.
  5. Send the sticker as it is, or add it to a set. To add it, choose the emoji that match its mood; Telegram uses AI to suggest some.

You can also put existing stickers into your own sets, for example to gather your favorites in one pack.

For animated or video stickers, you make the file in your own editor and upload it. Since August 2025 the @Stickers mini app handles this: it creates stickers and emoji, manages your packs and shows usage statistics. Core.telegram.org/stickers lists every file requirement, and for static stickers it suggests a transparent background, a white stroke and a black shadow so the sticker stands out.

How to remove Telegram stickers

Removing a set you added takes it off your list only. The set stays for its creator and for everyone else who added it. Back in 2015 Telegram described a Stickers menu where you view, uninstall and share your sets; its pages give no newer menu path for each app.

A few more things worth knowing:

  • Archived sets. When you add new sets, Telegram may archive old ones you haven't used. An archived set is not deleted; it just leaves the main list and can be brought back.
  • Recent stickers. You can remove one sticker from your recent list or clear the whole list. The Android app gained a button to clear all recent stickers at once in June 2022.
  • Favorites. As of 31 October 2026, Telegram's client configuration allows 5 favorite stickers, or 10 with Premium. A sticker can be removed from favorites.
  • Your own sets. The creator can delete a set they made, remove single stickers from it, replace them or change their order. You manage your packs in the @Stickers mini app.

The sticker in an empty chat

When someone opens a private chat with you and you have never talked, Telegram shows them a greeting sticker and an invitation to say hello. By default that sticker is picked at random. Telegram Business, which Telegram's developer documentation says is currently included with Premium, lets you set your own text and sticker for that empty chat instead. You find it under Settings > Telegram Business, according to Telegram's March 2024 post.

Stickers and Telegram gifts

People often mix up stickers and gifts, since both are bits of animated art you send to someone. They are separate features, and this page covers only stickers. Gifts have their own rules, which we explain in our guide to what Telegram gifts are. The currency used inside Telegram is covered in what Telegram Stars are. Questions people ask

Are Telegram stickers free?

Yes. Telegram's Premium FAQ lists tens of thousands of stickers among the features that stay free. Premium adds a set of stickers with full-screen effects and custom emoji sets, but nobody pays to send ordinary stickers.

How do I add a sticker set on Telegram?

Tap any sticker from that set in a chat and Telegram offers to add the whole set. Every set also has its own t.me link that opens it.

How do I make my own Telegram stickers?

Open the sticker panel in any chat and tap (+) to start the built-in sticker editor, then turn a photo into a sticker. For video or animated stickers, prepare a file that meets Telegram's requirements and upload it with the @Stickers mini app.

Can people without Premium see Premium stickers?

Yes. Telegram says the full-screen animations of Premium stickers play for all users; only sending them needs a subscription.

How many favorite stickers can I save?

As of 31 October 2026, Telegram's client configuration allows 5 favorite stickers without Premium and 10 with it.
