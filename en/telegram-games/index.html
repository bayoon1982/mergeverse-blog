Telegram games are played inside the Telegram app, with nothing extra to install. They come in two kinds: HTML5 games that a bot sends to a chat with a Play button, and mini apps, full applications that can take over the whole screen. Bot games can be played alone or against friends in a group, with a high-score table for each chat. If a game sells digital items, you pay in Telegram Stars.

What Telegram games are

Every Telegram game runs on a bot. Telegram's developer pages describe games as a type of content a bot can send, and its FAQ describes mini apps as applications built by outside developers on top of the bot platform, "from games and shops to AI tools". Telegram does not make the games itself.

The gaming platform launched on 3 October 2016. Telegram's blog announced about 30 games ready to play that day, most of them from @gamee, and promised that games would take no disk space, because they load on demand like web pages. If you are new to bots in general, our guide to Telegram bots is a good first stop.

Telegram's game documentation says that since April 2022 developers can also use Web Apps, today called mini apps, to build games in JavaScript. The November 2024 mini apps update added full-screen mode in both orientations, home screen shortcuts and device motion tracking, which the blog says allows games with gesture and tilt controls.

How to play a game on Telegram

For an HTML5 bot game:

  1. Open the game's bot, or a game message someone forwarded to you.
  2. Tap the Play button. Telegram's documentation says the first button on a game message is always the one that launches the game.
  3. The game opens in Telegram's in-app browser.
  4. When you finish, your score can go into that chat's high-score table.

For a mini app game:

  1. Open it from the bot's button, a link, or the Apps tab in Search.
  2. Let it go full screen, or turn your phone sideways if the game asks.
  3. If you play often, add a shortcut to your phone's home screen to open it in one tap.

A game can also have its own link of the form t.me/botname?game=gamename. Opening it lets you pick a chat and send the game there.

Playing with friends in groups

Competition across chats was the point of the original platform. Telegram's docs say bots can offer games to play solo or to compete in groups and one-on-one chats. The game message shows high scores for that chat; when someone sets a new record, a service message appears and the scoreboard updates. The 2016 blog post adds that the other players in the chat are told they need to step it up.

To share a game, use the standard share button in the top right corner of the game screen. Some games add their own button that sends the game to a chat together with your best score. You can also type a game bot's @username in the message field of any chat and pick a game from the list, a trick Telegram's blog showed with @gamee.

A group keeps the conversation going between rounds; see our guide to Telegram groups.

Where to find games

Telegram's FAQ names one official place: the Apps tab in Search, which shows popular mini apps. The developer pages give @gamebot and @gamee as examples of bot games.

For anything else, search for the game bot's exact username and compare it letter by letter before you start it, so you don't end up in a look-alike. How Telegram's search works is covered in Telegram search.

Paying inside games: Telegram Stars

Telegram's blog calls its bot platform free, but a developer can sell things inside a game. For digital goods and services, Telegram's payment rules say every transaction must be in Telegram Stars. The bot sends an invoice with a Pay button and you confirm the amount on Telegram's own payment screen. Stars are bought from Telegram through Apple or Google in-app purchases, or through @PremiumBot; our guide to what Telegram Stars are explains the rest.

If a purchase goes wrong, the developer is responsible, not Telegram. Telegram requires game bots and mini apps to answer a /paysupport command and lets developers refund payments in Stars. Bots are also asked to show their terms, usually through /terms, and you confirm them before you pay.

Telegram's November 2024 update also lets mini apps send you Telegram gifts. Who can put gifts on your profile is set in Settings > Privacy and Security > Gifts. What gifts are is explained in what Telegram gifts are.

What a game knows about you

When you open a mini app, Telegram's FAQ says its developer receives your name, username, profile picture, language and whether you have Premium, and sees your IP address like any website. Your phone number, location and messages are shared only if you choose to share them. Location needs your permission for each app separately. A mini app can also set your emoji status while you play, again only with your permission.

The FAQ's general advice for bots applies to every game: treat it like a stranger. Don't give it your passwords, your Telegram login codes or bank account numbers, even if it asks nicely, and be careful with files it sends.

Questions people ask

How do you play games on Telegram?

Open the game's bot or its t.me link and tap the Play button on the game message, or the button that opens its mini app. The game loads inside Telegram, so there is nothing to download.

Can I play Telegram games with friends?

Yes, with bot games. Telegram's documentation says bots can offer HTML5 games to play solo or to compete in groups and one-on-one chats, and the game message keeps high scores for each chat.

Where can I find games on Telegram?

Telegram's FAQ says the Apps tab in Search shows popular mini apps. Its developer pages name @gamebot and @gamee as examples of bot games, and you can search for any game bot by its exact username.

Are Telegram games free?

Telegram's blog calls the bot platform free, but each developer decides whether to sell anything inside a game. If they sell digital items, Telegram's rules say the payment must be in Telegram Stars.

Does Telegram make these games?

No. Telegram's FAQ says mini apps are built by outside developers on Telegram's bot platform, not by Telegram. Treat them the way you would any bot.
