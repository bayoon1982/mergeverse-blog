In short

  • The search box above your chat list looks through your chats, your messages and public usernames at once.
  • You can find a person by username without knowing their phone number; whether they can find you by number depends on your privacy settings.
  • Inside a chat you can search messages, filter by sender and tap a hashtag to see every post that uses it.
  • Since July 2025 a Posts tab searches public channels; it started with Premium and, per Telegram's developer docs, extra searches beyond the free daily ones cost Stars.
  • Removing your username takes you out of global search without touching existing chats.

Telegram search is the box above your chat list. Type a word and it looks through your chats and messages and through public usernames across Telegram, so it finds people, channels, groups and bots you have never talked to. Tabs narrow the results to channels, apps, media, links, files, music or voice messages. Inside any chat you can search its messages, filter by sender and tap hashtags.

All of this comes from telegram.org and core.telegram.org, checked on 31 October 2026. We are not affiliated with Telegram, and we don't recommend outside search sites or search bots.

What the search box finds

Telegram's FAQ describes it as a universal search box: one field for chats, messages and usernames. When you type, results come in two kinds.

The first kind is your own stuff: chats you're in, contacts, and messages from your chats. The second is global results: public users, channels, groups and bots whose username or name matches what you typed, even if you have never seen them.

Before you type anything, the Chats tab shows two rows, according to Telegram's developer guidelines: frequent contacts and recent chats. That's why the people you write to most sit at the top.

How to find a user on Telegram

You have three ways to reach someone, and only one of them needs their phone number.

  1. By username. If the person set a public username, type it into search. Telegram says they appear under "global results". A username can contain a-z, 0-9 and underscores, and capital letters don't matter: Telegram and TeleGram are the same user.
  2. By link. A username also works as a link, t.me/username or username.t.me, which opens a chat with that person straight from any app.
  3. By phone number. Contacts in your address book who use Telegram show up automatically. A link like t.me/+ followed by a number in international format also opens a chat, but only if that person's privacy settings let others find them by number.

People who have no username and aren't in your contacts can still be reached in one case: when you're both members of the same group. Telegram's FAQ adds that neither side sees the other's phone number unless privacy settings allow it.

Our guide to Telegram usernames covers picking and changing one, and the page on your Telegram ID explains the number behind every account.

Finding channels, groups and bots

Public channels and groups have usernames too, so they show up in global results the same way. Private ones don't: you need an invite link.

Telegram's developer guidelines describe more tabs in search. The exact set can differ between apps and versions, but here is what Telegram documents:

Tab What it shows
Chats Your chats and messages, plus public users, groups and channels
Channels Channels you joined and similar public channels; typing searches channels only
Apps Mini apps of bots, the Mini App Store
Posts Posts from public channels
Media Photos and videos from your chats
Downloads Files you downloaded, searched on your device
Links, Files, Music, Voice Messages of that type from your chats

The Channels tab also suggests similar channels. Telegram added those in November 2023, chosen by overlap in subscriber bases. As of 31 October 2026, the client configuration allows up to 10 suggestions, or 100 with Premium. We explain joining and muting in our guide to Telegram channels and the same for Telegram groups.

Searching posts in public channels

Since 31 July 2025, search has a Posts tab with results from public channels, including ones you haven't joined. Telegram's announcement said it was "initially available" to Premium users.

Telegram's developer documentation adds how it works today. Post searches are paid: Premium users get a number of free searches a day, and after that each search costs Telegram Stars. The app shows how many free searches are left and when more become available. That's also why a post search starts only when you press a button, while ordinary search updates as you type. Our page on what Telegram Stars are explains the currency.

Searching messages inside a chat

Every chat has its own search. Telegram's FAQ says you can find a message "even among millions" and filter by sender, which helps in a busy group.

You can also browse a chat by type. The developer documentation lists filters for photos, videos, files, links, GIFs, voice messages, music, round videos, pinned messages and messages that mention you. In the apps, these appear as the media, files and links sections of a chat's profile.

Steps that work in any chat:

  1. Open the chat.
  2. Open its search from the chat's header or menu.
  3. Type a word, or pick a sender to see only their messages.
  4. Tap a result to jump to it in the conversation.

Telegram's pages don't give a button path for each app, so the icon may sit in a slightly different place on your phone or computer.

Hashtags in Telegram search

Tap a hashtag in a message and Telegram opens search with every other message in that chat that uses the same tag. Channel owners use this to sort old posts: a tag per topic works like a menu.

Telegram's developer documentation describes a wider hashtag search too. It looks through posts from public channels, including ones you haven't joined, and stories from public accounts. Cashtags, which start with $ instead of #, open the same kind of search inside a chat.

Stickers, GIFs and emoji

Search also works inside the panels where you pick stickers, GIFs and emoji.

  • Emoji. Type ":" followed by a keyword in the message field, for example :heart, to search emoji.
  • Stickers and emoji. Since May 2026 the sticker panel searches more than 100 million emoji and stickers made by Telegram users, in 36 languages.
  • GIFs. Since July 2026 the GIF panel searches over 350 million GIFs in 36 languages. Telegram says these searches don't rely on any third parties.

How to stay out of Telegram search

If you'd rather not be found, the username is the switch. Telegram's FAQ is blunt: a public username lets anyone find you in global search and message you, even without your number. To remove it, go to Settings and save an empty username. People can no longer find you through search, and your existing chats stay as they are.

Your phone number is a separate setting. By default only people who have you in their contacts see it, and you can change who can find you by number under Settings, Privacy and Security, Phone Number.

Search only decides who can find you. What people see once they open your profile is a separate question; for gifts on your profile, see how to hide Telegram gifts.

Questions people ask

How do I search for a user on Telegram?

Type their username into the search box above your chat list. If they have set a public username, they appear under global results. People without a username can be found only through a shared group, your contacts or a phone number their privacy settings allow.

Does Telegram have a built-in search engine?

Yes. The search box finds public channels, groups, bots and users by name or username, searches your own messages, and has tabs for media, links, files, music and voice messages. A Posts tab also searches public channel posts.

Can people find me on Telegram without my phone number?

Only if you set a public username or share a group with them. Telegram says that once you have a username, anyone who finds you can message you, so skip it if you'd rather not be found.

How do I search messages in a Telegram chat?

Open the chat and use its search. Telegram's FAQ says you can filter results by sender, and tapping a hashtag in a message opens a search for every message with that tag in the chat.

Is Telegram search private?

Telegram says its GIF search does not rely on third parties and its AI-powered sticker search runs on its own Cocoon network. Its pages don't publish a broader statement about other search queries.
