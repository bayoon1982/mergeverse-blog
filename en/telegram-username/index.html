A Telegram username is an optional public name, like @example, that lets people find you in search and message you without knowing your phone number. As of 31 October 2026 it can be 5 to 32 characters long and use only Latin letters a-z, digits 0-9 and underscores. You set, change or remove it in Settings, and it gives you a link, t.me/username, that opens a chat with you.

Every rule below comes from telegram.org and core.telegram.org, read on 31 October 2026. This site is not affiliated with Telegram.

What a Telegram username is

Your Telegram account has three different identifiers, and they are easy to mix up. Your phone number is what you sign up with. Your user ID is a number Telegram assigns behind the scenes (we explain it in what a Telegram ID is). Your username is the only one you choose yourself, and the only one meant to be public.

The FAQ puts it simply: once you set a public username, other users can find you by it. Telegram's privacy policy goes further and says a username, once set, is always public. Groups, channels and bots can have usernames too. That is how a public channel gets an address like t.me/telegram.

A username is optional. Plenty of people never set one and use Telegram only with their contacts.

Telegram username rules: characters and length

Telegram's FAQ lists the characters, and the API documentation for changing a username adds the length. As of 31 October 2026:

Rule What Telegram allows
Length 5 to 32 characters
Letters Latin a-z only
Digits 0-9
Symbols Underscore (_) only
Capital letters Ignored when matching: @Example and @example are the same name
Who gets a name Whoever claims a free name first

Spaces, dots, hyphens, emoji and non-Latin letters are not accepted, and capitalization does not change the name.

Bots follow the same 5 to 32 character rule, and a bot's username must end in "bot", according to Telegram's bot documentation. That suffix is a quick way to tell a bot from a person, though a few old official bots such as @gif do without it. We cover bots in Telegram bots explained.

How to set or change your Telegram username

The username field lives in your profile settings in every official app. The FAQ says only "in Settings" and does not give a separate path for each app, so here are the general steps:

  1. Open Telegram and go to Settings.
  2. Open your profile details (on most apps, tap your name or the edit option at the top).
  3. Tap the Username field.
  4. Type the name you want. Telegram checks it as you type and tells you if it is taken or not valid.
  5. Save.

Changing a username works the same way: type a new name over the old one and save. Keep in mind that the old name stops pointing to you. Anyone with your old link will no longer reach you through it, so update the name anywhere you shared it.

If the name you want is taken, Telegram's FAQ explains that basic usernames go to whoever claims them first. It may reclaim names held by bots and channels that are not in use, and openly squatted names. To ask about a specific name, the FAQ points to @Username_bot.

The Telegram username link

Once you have a username, you have a link. The FAQ describes two formats:

  • t.me/username
  • username.t.me

Both open the Telegram app and start a chat with you. Telegram's link documentation says the second form should be treated exactly like the first. You can put the link in an email signature, on a website or on a business card, and the person tapping it does not need your number.

If you want people to reach you by phone number instead, Telegram also supports links like t.me/+123456789, with the number in international format. The FAQ adds a condition: that link works only if your privacy settings let others find you by your phone number.

Why a Telegram username is not found

When you search for a name or open a t.me link and Telegram says it can't find the user, the cause is usually one of these:

  • A typo. Check every character. Underscores are easy to drop, and a lookalike letter such as "l" in place of "I" won't match.
  • The owner changed the name. The old name no longer leads to them.
  • The owner removed the name. The FAQ says that after a username is removed, people can no longer find that account via search.
  • The name was reclaimed. Telegram may take back names from unused bots and channels.
  • The name is shorter than 5 characters or uses a symbol Telegram does not allow, so it was never a valid ordinary username.

If a link was given to you by someone you don't know, be careful with any account it opens, even when it looks right. Copying a well-known name with one letter changed is an old trick.

Privacy: who can find you by username

A username makes you findable. The FAQ says others can use Global Search and find you by your username, and then message you even if they don't have your number.

What they don't get is your phone number. By default your number is visible only to people who saved you as a contact, and you control that under Settings > Privacy and Security > Phone Number. Someone who found you by username sees your number only if those settings allow it. People who already had your number saved will still see it.

Bots see your username too. Telegram's FAQ says bots can see your public name, username and profile pictures, plus the messages you send them, but not your phone number unless you share it.

If you don't want strangers to write to you, the simplest choice is not to set a username at all. The same goes for anything else on your profile: if gifts are part of it, see how to hide Telegram gifts.

If someone uses a name that copies yours to pretend to be you, the FAQ says to contact @NoToScam.

How to remove your Telegram username

Removing a username takes one step: go to the username field in Settings, delete the name so the field is empty, and save.

Here is what the FAQ says happens next:

  • People can no longer find you through search.
  • Your existing conversations are not affected. Everyone you already talk to keeps the chat.
  • Your t.me link stops working, because there is no name behind it.

Telegram's pages don't say how soon a removed name can be claimed by someone else, so if you might want it back, keep it.

Collectible usernames on Fragment

Alongside ordinary usernames there are collectible ones. Telegram announced them on 5 November 2022. According to that post, ownership of collectible usernames is secured by TON, a blockchain network, and they are handled through a separate platform at fragment.com. The same post says collectible usernames can be assigned to accounts and public chats.

Telegram's API also has a dedicated response for a name that is not free to claim but is available on fragment.com, so if Telegram tells you a name is on Fragment, that is what it means. This site does not cover trading names or their prices. If you want the collectible side of Telegram in general, our pages on what Telegram gifts are and Telegram NFT gifts explain the gift equivalent, and what Telegram Stars are covers Telegram's in-app currency. For the profile basics, ordinary usernames are free, and nothing about them requires Fragment.

Questions people ask

How long can a Telegram username be?

As of 31 October 2026, Telegram's API documentation gives a length of 5 to 32 characters. Accepted characters are a-z (case-insensitive), 0-9 and underscores.

Can someone see my phone number if they find me by username?

No, unless your privacy settings allow it. Telegram's FAQ says people who find you by username can message you without seeing your number. Who sees your number is set under Settings > Privacy and Security > Phone Number.

Why does a Telegram username link say the user was not found?

Usually the name is mistyped, or its owner changed or removed it. Telegram's FAQ says that after a username is removed, people can no longer find that account through search. Telegram may also reclaim names held by unused bots and channels.

Are Telegram usernames case-sensitive?

No. @Example and @example are the same name. Telegram stores the capitalization you typed and shows it on your profile.

Do I need a username to use Telegram?

No. A username is optional. Without one, people can reach you only through your phone number, if your settings allow that, or through chats and groups you share.
