In short

  • Only gifts that Telegram marks as upgradable can be upgraded; the option simply is not there on the others.
  • The price is in Stars and changes over time, so the app's number is the one to trust.
  • If the sender paid for the upgrade, it costs you nothing. Anyone can also prepay someone else's upgrade.
  • An upgraded gift can be worn, sold, sent on, moved to the blockchain or crafted, but no longer converted into Stars.
  • You choose whether the sender's name and message stay on the collectible; removing them later costs Stars.

To upgrade a Telegram gift, open your profile, go to Gifts, tap the gift and choose the upgrade. You pay a price in Stars, which the app shows before you confirm, or nothing if the sender already paid for it. The gift turns into a collectible with its own number and random traits. Only gifts Telegram marks as upgradable have the option at all.

What upgrading a gift does

Telegram introduced upgrades on 1 January 2025. Its blog describes the result: the gift gets a new appearance chosen from dozens of variations drawn by Telegram's artists, plus a random set of secondary traits, a background colour, an icon and a number. In the app and in the API these traits are called model, backdrop and symbol. Some combinations come up far less often than others; each collectible's page shows how rare each trait is as a percentage.

After the upgrade the gift is a collectible. That changes what you can do with it:

Before (regular gift) After (collectible)
Show on your profile Yes Yes
Convert into Stars Within 7 days of receiving it No
Sell on Telegram's marketplace No Yes
Wear as your emoji status No Yes
Move to the TON blockchain No Yes
Use in crafting No Yes

The biggest trade-off is the second row. Once upgraded, a gift can no longer be converted into Stars. If Stars are what you want, decide before you upgrade.

Which gifts can be upgraded?

Not all of them. Telegram's API documentation shows that a received gift can be upgraded only when Telegram has enabled that for it, and that each upgradable gift carries its own upgrade price. At launch Telegram listed more than 20 gifts that could already be upgraded, among them Homemade Cake, Jelly Bunny, Spiced Wine and Santa Hat. Telegram has added gifts since then, so that list is history, not a current catalogue.

If the gift you hold has no upgrade option, there is no hidden setting that adds one. Our explainer on what Telegram gifts are covers the kinds of gifts and why some of them stay regular.

How to upgrade a gift, step by step

Telegram's posts give the path to your gifts (My Profile, then Gifts) but do not print the label of the upgrade button. The steps below follow that path; the button text in your app may differ slightly between iPhone, Android and desktop.

  1. Open your profile and go to the Gifts tab.
  2. Tap the gift you want to upgrade.
  3. Choose the upgrade option. If it is missing, the gift cannot be upgraded.
  4. Decide whether to keep the sender's name and message on the collectible (see below).
  5. Check the price in Stars and confirm. If the upgrade was prepaid, there is nothing to pay.

The gift then opens with its new look, number and traits. You cannot pick the traits: they are assigned at random.

How much does the upgrade cost?

The upgrade is paid in Stars. Telegram's January 2025 post said the Stars cover blockchain fees. The API documentation adds a detail most guides miss: the upgrade price changes over time, and the app can show upcoming prices before you pay.

That is why we print no fixed number. Guides from early 2025 quote a specific price; we could not find it on any current Telegram page, and the documentation says it moves. Trust the number in your app on the day you upgrade.

Upgrading for free when the sender paid

When buying a gift, the sender can include the upgrade. You then receive a gift you can turn into a collectible without paying anything. In January 2025 Telegram also let channel subscribers pay for the upgrade before sending a gift to a channel, so the channel can upgrade it free.

Telegram's API documentation goes one step further: anyone can prepay the upgrade of a gift someone else holds. If a friend offers to cover your upgrade, that is a real feature, not a trick. You still choose when to upgrade.

Keeping or removing the sender's name and message

A regular gift carries who sent it and the note they wrote. When you upgrade, you can keep those details on the collectible. Telegram's documentation says they are then stored with the gift as an attribute, and that removing them later costs Stars.

Think about it if you may sell the gift one day: whoever buys it will see what was kept. Since August 2026 buyers on the marketplace can also add their own comment when sending a gift ("signed gifts", per Telegram's blog).

After the upgrade

A collectible opens up options a regular gift does not have:

  • Wear it. Telegram's January 2025 post says a collectible can be your emoji status, and your profile takes on its design (My Profile, Gifts, tap the gift, Wear).
  • Sell it on Telegram's marketplace for Stars or TON. Our guide on how to sell a Telegram gift has the steps and the documented seller's share.
  • Move it to the TON blockchain, where it becomes an NFT you control outside Telegram. Our page on Telegram NFT gifts explains what that means.
  • Craft it. Since February 2026 Telegram lets you combine up to four collectibles of the same gift into an Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary version. Success is a matter of chance, and Telegram's API documentation says every gift used is burned whether the craft succeeds or fails.

When the upgrade button is missing

The documented reasons are few:

  • The gift is not upgradable. Telegram decides this per gift.
  • The gift was already converted into Stars, so there is nothing left to upgrade.
  • It is already a collectible.

There is also the plain one: an old app. Telegram's August 2026 post shows how to switch on automatic updates (on Android: Play Store, Settings, Network Preferences, Auto-update Apps; on iPhone: Settings, Apps, App Store, App Updates).

Questions people ask

Why can't I upgrade my Telegram gift?

Not every gift can be upgraded: Telegram enables the option per gift. The gift may also have been converted into Stars already, or be a collectible already. It is also worth updating the app before you look again.

How much does it cost to upgrade a Telegram gift?

It is paid in Stars, and Telegram's API documentation says the price changes over time; the app shows it before you confirm. We do not quote a fixed number: older guides cite figures that are no longer reliable.

Can I upgrade a gift for free?

Yes, if the sender included the upgrade when sending, or if someone else prepaid it for you. Then the upgrade costs you no Stars.

Can I convert an upgraded gift back into Stars?

No. Conversion into Stars is described only for regular gifts. A collectible can be sold on Telegram's marketplace instead.

Can I upgrade a gift twice?

There is nothing to upgrade further: an upgraded gift is already a collectible. Since February 2026 Telegram's crafting combines collectibles into rarer versions, which is a different process.
